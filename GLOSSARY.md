# svoboda.page

Personal presentation site of its owner, a junior frontend developer who works with AI agents. It exists to get the owner hired.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of work the owner built, shown on the site with its purpose, role, technologies and links.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item, repo

**Featured Project**:
One of the two or three Projects highlighted at the top of the home page.
_Avoid_: Top project, highlight

**Case Study**:
A Project's own page, telling its story as problem → decisions → result. Every Project has one.
_Avoid_: Project detail, article

**Timeline**:
The chronological path of the owner's learning and work: a sequence of Milestones, each holding its Projects.
_Avoid_: Project tree, journey, history

**Milestone**:
A dated chapter of the owner's path (a course, a job, a learning period, including work before programming). Every Project belongs to exactly one Milestone; a Milestone may have none.
_Avoid_: Chapter, stage, event, experience

**Skill**:
A technology or ability the owner claims, with a self-assessed level and the Projects that prove it.
_Avoid_: Tech, stack item, competency

**CV**:
A printable summary of the owner, derived from the same content as the site.
_Avoid_: Resume, Životopis

**Intro Video**:
A short self-introduction video by the owner, planned for after the first release.
_Avoid_: Promo, showreel
